# Community Code of Conduct

We want Namma Kelasa to be a safe, welcoming project for contributors of every background and experience level.

## Expected behaviour

- Communicate respectfully and focus feedback on the work.
- Welcome Kannada and English participation.
- Protect job seekers, employers, and contributors from harassment or discrimination.
- Respect privacy and never publish personal, résumé, authentication, or payment data.
- Prefer constructive, specific suggestions and assume good intent.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, hate speech, threats, impersonation, spam, scams, credential sharing, and disclosure of another person's private information are not permitted.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
